## tui-project-archive-vs-tree-archive

- Left-tree `ArchiveRoot` is the project-list expander for archived issue rows.
- Project-detail `Archive` is a summary panel titled `Archived Issues`.
- Project-detail archive copy must not mention `h/l` or the left-tree archive row.
- Tree archive summary owns the `h/l expands or collapses archived issue rows` hint.
