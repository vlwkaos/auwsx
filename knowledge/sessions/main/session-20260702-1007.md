## tui-issue-section-enter-inspect

- Corrected issue-detail semantics: Enter is section inspection for Summary, Findings, Subtasks / Queue, and Log.
- Selection keys remain `j/k` and `h/l` while the section is inactive.
- Active section keys are shared: `j/k`, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End scroll; Esc exits active section mode.
- Added `App::issue_section_scroll` for non-log issue section scroll state. Log keeps `issue_log_scroll`.
- Overflow hints now say `enter section to scroll` for issue panels and the footer says `(Enter) inspect`.
